"Not a hotel that happens to be near the lake — a lake house that happens to take guests."

The Serai sits directly on Dal Lake, a five-minute shikara ride from the old town. Nine rooms, each facing water or garden, furnished in walnut and local wool. No two are exactly alike — we built this from a family home, not a blueprint.
