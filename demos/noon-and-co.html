Our Story

We just wanted a place to sit with good chai.

Noon & Co. started as a two-table setup with one flask of noon chai and a lot of opinions about how it should taste. It grew into a full cafe, but the chai recipe never changed. Everything here is made in-house, most days by the same three people who opened it.
